FIELD MANUAL // BLACK BOX AQUARIUM

Premise

12 drops of alien ocean. Keep them alive. Evolution is emergent, not scripted. Traits are heritable. Appearance derives from genome. No creature is pre-designed.

Environment

Temperature, Oxygen, pH, Light, Nutrients, Contamination interact. Producers need light+nuts. Consumers need oxygen. Contamination kills. Filter mitigates. Baseline inflow prevents trivial collapse but overgrazing can still crash.

Ecology

  • Producer: photosynthesizes, base of chain, health regen
  • Grazer: eats producers, less lethal grazing
  • Filter: eats nutrients/particles, stable
  • Scavenger: eats detritus from dead
  • Predator: hunts smaller, armor check
  • Parasite/Symbiote: attaches, drains or shares if social high

Failure / Recovery

If all die, failure overlay offers restart or load. Auto-reseed triggers if producers <2 or total <4. Seed producers manually, lower temp extremes, add nutrients.

Traits (heritable)

size, speed, metabolism, tempPref, armor, sensory, reproRate, aggression, social, biolum, hue, saturation, bodyPlan, fins, appendages, shell, eyes, mouth, markings, feeding. All bounded, mutated ±18% range, 6% big jump.

Player Tools

Influence, not command. Change environment, feed, place habitat, introduce/cull, use isolation tank for selective breeding. Selection pressure alters population: cold breeds cold-resistant, low light selects efficient producers.

Research

Observation unlocks traits. Species catalog clusters similar genomes. Graphs show population. Lineage shows mutations with from→to.
