FIELD MANUAL // BLACK BOX AQUARIUM

Premise

12 drops of alien ocean. Keep them alive. Evolution is emergent, not scripted. Traits are heritable. Appearance derives from genome. No creature is pre-designed.

Environment

Temperature, Oxygen, pH, Light, Nutrients, Contamination interact. Producers need light+nuts. Consumers need oxygen. Contamination kills. Filter mitigates.

Ecology

  • Producer: photosynthesizes, base of chain
  • Grazer: eats producers
  • Filter: eats nutrients/particles
  • Scavenger: eats detritus
  • Predator: hunts others
  • Parasite/Symbiote: attaches, drains or shares

Traits

size, speed, metabolism, tempPref, armor, sensory, reproRate, aggression, social, bodyPlan, fins, appendages, shell, eyes, mouth, markings, biolum. All heritable with mutation.

Player Tools

Influence, not command. Change environment, feed, place habitat, introduce/cull, use isolation tank for selective breeding. Selection pressure alters population.

Research

Observation unlocks traits. Species catalog clusters similar genomes. Graphs show population. Lineage shows mutations.
